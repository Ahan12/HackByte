About Trash2Taste

Trash2Taste is an innovative platform dedicated to reducing food waste through advanced AI and sophisticated meal planning. Our system generates optimized menus and collects precise wastage data using YOLO image classification — from inside a trashcan — to continually refine our predictive model via transfer learning.

By merging machine learning with real–world data, we ensure kitchens serve exactly what is needed. Experience a streamlined, high–tech approach to food service.

Our Vision & Technology

Our Vision

To revolutionize food service operations by leveraging AI for precision in meal planning and waste reduction.

Our Technology

Through YOLO image classification and transfer learning, we continuously refine our model, ensuring every meal is perfectly planned.

How It Works

Our system combines advanced machine learning with YOLO image classification to monitor food waste across categories. This data refines our menu suggestions and minimizes leftovers.

  • Anticipate daily meal demand accurately
  • Reduce overproduction and waste
  • Analyze real–time data to improve future menus
